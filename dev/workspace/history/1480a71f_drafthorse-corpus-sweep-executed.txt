**Why I kept Fable everywhere:**
- Ultracode active — session directive says correctness over token cost. Default stands unless confident a lower tier fits.
- The sweep's core is judgment, not substitution. Plans say so explicitly: de-hold reshaping is "not a pattern delete — each site is a judgment call"; condition splits are "not mechanical... every split is a judgment about what counts as one look". Wrong trim = silent routing break in a shipped skill.
- #39 is the cautionary tale in your own repo: an auditor (git-robot-class docs run `model: sonnet`; saddler unpinned) held a check verbatim and still missed it — set-level semantic judgment degrades first on smaller models. Same failure mode threatens migration judgment.

**Honest counterpoint** — my briefs pre-decided most judgment (versioning per-step, git-box `+Conclude`, `+Stash` behaviour, de-hold classes). With briefs that tight, roughly 8 of 13 assignments (verb skills, small agents, handovers) were Sonnet-safe. Fable-worthy regardless: classroom (largest, most open calls), versioning (interacting fixes), spec-check + saddler regeneration, #42 design work.

**Rule of thumb for next time** — spend the strong model where judgment concentrates: brief-writing (done in main loop), the hard 4–5 documents, and especially the **verify/audit pass** — cheaper to migrate on Sonnet and audit on Fable than the reverse. Mechanical fan-out (greps, inventories, citation conversion) → `sonnet` or `haiku` + `effort: 'low'`.
