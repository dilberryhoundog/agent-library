18

The Paper/Ink palette is already canonical and needs no design work: prompt-builder's 45 token declarations (14 colour/shadow tokens x 3 theme blocks, plus 3 type tokens) are byte-identical to paper-ink-fingerprint's, and five of the seven in-family files carry the same values. Five feature-list surfaces have no tokens anywhere in the corpus: a diff gutter, a keep/context colour, filter-tag colours, a labelled quote block, and a recommended-option highlight; the cards name the exact tokens for each.
